Methods & Limitations

Variants were classified using an implementation of the ACMG/AMP 2015 combining-rules framework (Richards et al., Genet Med 2015). Evidence codes were derived automatically from three sources: ClinVar (PP5/BP6), gnomAD population allele frequency (BA1/BS1/PM2), and VEP consequence annotation gated by a curated gene-mechanism list (PVS1, applied only to genes with an established loss-of-function disease mechanism).

This is a research and portfolio tool, not a validated clinical assay. Known limitations:
  • Automated evidence extraction currently covers a subset of ACMG/AMP criteria (PP5, BP6, BA1, BS1, PM2, PVS1). Criteria requiring segregation data, functional studies, or de novo confirmation (PS2, PS3, PP1, etc.) are not automated and would need manual curation.
  • PM2 (rarity) reflects absence from gnomAD's public API at the time of the query, not a formally reviewed population frequency threshold specific to each gene/disease.
  • PVS1 is gated to a small, manually curated list of genes with established loss-of-function mechanisms; genes not on that list will not receive PVS1 evidence regardless of consequence, by design.
  • ClinVar-derived evidence (PP5/BP6) reflects a third party's classification, not independent evidence review.
